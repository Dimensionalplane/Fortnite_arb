# VISION

## Core Goal
To build a highly efficient, automated, and legally compliant Digital Asset Arbitrage system.

## Foundational Concepts
- **Digital Ownership**: Prioritizing platforms that support true user-to-user asset trading (e.g., Steam Market).
- **Automation**: Using high-volume scanning and analysis to identify undervalued assets.
- **Compliance**: Adhering strictly to Terms of Service (ToS) to ensure long-term viability and protect assets.

## User-Satisfaction Design
- Clear, actionable insights for arbitrage opportunities.
- Robust tracking of asset value over time.
- Seamless integration with supported marketplaces.
